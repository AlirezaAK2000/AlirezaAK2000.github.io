**Independent open-source project**

I implemented a preference-alignment pipeline for Llama 3 using Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and Odds Ratio Preference Optimization (ORPO). Fine-tuning uses Unsloth and LoRA, and a trained reward model is used to assess alignment with response preferences.

**Tools and methods:** Python, Llama 3, DPO, ORPO, LoRA, Unsloth, reward-model evaluation.
