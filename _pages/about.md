I am a final-year M.Sc. student in **Artificial Intelligence and Robotics** at the **University of Tehran**, affiliated with the **Cognitive Systems Lab**. My research asks how explicit knowledge from large language models can help reinforcement learning agents learn more efficiently. I am advised by Prof. Majid Nili Ahmadabadi and Prof. Babak Nadjar Araabi.

In my [master's research]({{ '/projects/llm-fuzzy-priors/' | relative_url }}) investigates how knowledge generated by large language models can improve the sample efficiency of reinforcement learning agents. I translate linguistic guidance into fuzzy controllers and study how agents can decide when to rely on this guidance and when to use their learned policies. I am particularly interested in how agents can benefit from imperfect prior knowledge while accounting for its uncertainty and limitations.

Alongside research, I build LLM applications using tools such as MCP, knowledge graphs, and ontologies. My industry experience spans backend development, data engineering, and information retrieval.

I also enjoy teaching and creating educational content. I have enjoyed serving as a teaching assistant across eight university courses, and I share English-language tutorials on building LLM applications on my YouTube channel,[The AI Tutorial Guy](https://www.youtube.com/@TheAITutorialGuy).

### Research interests

- **Reinforcement learning:** sample efficiency, continuous control, and learning with prior knowledge.
- **LLMs and structured knowledge:** executable policy priors, and knowledge-grounded agents.
- **Fuzzy Logic:** fuzzy control
- **Empirical research:** multi-seed experiments, uncertainty-aware decisions, and reproducible evaluation.
