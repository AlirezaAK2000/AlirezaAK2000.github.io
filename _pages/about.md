I am a final-year M.Sc. student in **Artificial Intelligence and Robotics** at the **University of Tehran**, affiliated with the **Cognitive Systems Lab**. My research asks how explicit knowledge from large language models can help reinforcement learning agents learn more efficiently. I am advised by Prof. Majid Nili Ahmadabadi and Prof. Babak Nadjar Araabi, and expect to graduate in **January 2027**.

In my [master's research]({{ '/projects/llm-fuzzy-priors/' | relative_url }}), I turn LLM-generated knowledge into executable fuzzy controllers and use uncertainty-aware arbitration to combine these priors with a learned TD3 policy. I evaluate the approach in MuJoCo and Meta-World. Previously, my bachelor's thesis at **Amirkabir University of Technology** explored reinforcement learning for pedestrian-aware driving in CARLA.

Alongside research, I build [LLM and knowledge-graph applications]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}), have worked in software engineering, and have contributed as a teaching assistant across eight university courses. I also share English-language LLM application tutorials on [The AI Tutorial Guy](https://www.youtube.com/@TheAITutorialGuy).

### Research interests

- **Reinforcement learning:** sample efficiency, continuous control, and learning with prior knowledge.
- **LLMs and structured knowledge:** executable policy priors, fuzzy control, and knowledge-grounded agents.
- **Empirical research:** multi-seed experiments, uncertainty-aware decisions, and reproducible evaluation.
